When you need help, here’s what happens.

Every request follows the same path, so nothing gets lost between people.

  1. SubmitSend an inquiry or report an issue.
  2. Sorted and assignedYour request is categorized and given to the right person.
  3. InvestigatedThe team looks into what happened.
  4. Resolved or escalatedYou get an answer, or it moves to the next level.
  5. DocumentedThe outcome is recorded.
  6. Follow-up and quality reviewWe check that it was resolved well.
